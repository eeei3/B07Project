EcoTracker

Track Your Carbon Footprint

EcoTracker helps you monitor your environmental impact by tracking transportation, food, and shopping emissions. Make informed decisions to reduce your carbon footprint and live more sustainably.

Key Features:

  • Track transportation emissions
  • Monitor food carbon footprint
  • Log shopping activities
  • Calendar-based activity tracking
  • Visual charts and progress tracking
  • Personalized recommendations
📱 Download APK

Installation Instructions:

  1. Download the APK file above
  2. On your Android device, go to Settings > Security
  3. Enable "Install from unknown sources" or "Allow from this source"
  4. Open the downloaded APK file
  5. Follow the installation prompts
  6. Open EcoTracker and start tracking!
Note: This app requires Android 7.0 (API level 24) or higher. Make sure to enable installation from unknown sources in your device settings before downloading.
